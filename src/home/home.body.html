A different face at every gate

Each site gets one complete, consistent browser identity for the whole visit. When a site pushes back, Svipall does not guess: it climbs to a stronger way of visiting. Pick a site to see where it gets in.

How it climbs

Five ways of visiting, from lightest to most patient.

  1. 1A plain requestFast, for pages with no wall—
  2. 2A hidden browserRuns the page like a browser would—
  3. 3A disguised browserHides the signs of automation—
  4. 4A real browserWith its own profile for that site—
  5. 5A patient visitWaits and answers the challenge—
Read

The page came back as clean text, ready for your AI.

Another way in

A wall went up, so it came back a stronger way and got in.

Refused, and said so

Some walls are not Svipall’s to pass: it never signs in for you. Your AI is told plainly, never handed a block screen as the answer.

What your AI can do with it

Watch a video for you

It hears what’s said and sees what’s shown, then zooms in on the moment you care about, even the small text on a slide.

Search without a subscription

It asks several search engines at once and keeps what they agree on. No key to buy, no monthly limit.

Keep watch while you’re away

Prices, stock, job posts, news. Your AI hears about it the moment the page changes.

Pages into spreadsheets

A list of products, places or results becomes rows you can sort, compare and download.

Open pages that block bots

When a site puts up a “checking your browser” screen, Svipall changes how it visits until it gets in.

It stays on your computer

Svipall runs on your own machine. Pages go straight from the site to you and on to your AI app. Nothing is sent to us, because there is no “us” in the middle.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Your AI does the setup from one message, and after that you just talk to it.

How is this different from the AI’s own web search?

It opens more sites, reads videos and tables, watches pages over time, and says clearly when it couldn’t open something.

Who sees what I look up?

Only the sites you visit and your own AI. Svipall runs on your computer and has no tracking.
